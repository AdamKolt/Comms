Commercialization at Scale

1 Portfolio Strategy & Market Creation

  • Runs market opportunity assessments — sizing addressable demand, evaluating first-mover windows, mapping competitive whitespace, and identifying regulated, adjacency, or platform-driven openings where a new commercial play can create durable value.
  • Builds investment-grade business cases — market sizing, revenue projections, unit economics, recurring-revenue contribution, and CAC/LTV models — that secure executive approval and capital commitment to launch new commercial motions.
  • Leads market creation and category creation — built Saber’s HAVA-compliant Elections Management practice from zero to $57.8M across 13 states, converting a federal compliance mandate into a repeatable commercial platform with recurring revenue streams.
  • Defines portfolio expansion strategy — positioning each new commercial offer (CCTV-as-a-Service, IdeaHub, Data Monetization, B2B2C Embedded Distribution) as the anchor of a broader managed-services or platform portfolio, with a reusable commercial wrapper for the adjacent offers that follow.
  • Identifies strategic adjacencies to the core business — managed security, workplace collaboration, data monetization, and embedded distribution — turning existing customer relationships into multi-product accounts at near-zero customer acquisition cost.
  • Translates corporate strategy into a commercialization roadmap — sequencing market entries, offer launches, and channel investments against revenue milestones rather than feature backlogs.

2 Offer Design & Commercialization

  • Owns end-to-end offer design — defining customer problem, value hypothesis, commercial packaging, pricing model, contract structure, and the buying experience — for national, local, partner, and platform offers.
  • Designs business model innovation — CAPEX-to-OPEX shifts, usage-based pricing, lease-to-own structures, recurring-revenue wraps around hardware, and bundled subscriptions that convert one-time sales into long-tail commercial relationships.
  • Builds pricing & packaging structures — tiered subscriptions, attach incentives, multi-year contract terms, and bundle architectures — that anchor price to value, protect margin, and lift ARPA on every account.
  • Runs launch readiness governance — cross-functional gating across Product, Sales, Marketing, Legal, Regulatory, Operations, Partner Management, and Finance; go/no-go reviews at every milestone; and post-launch audits that keep execution honest.
  • Defines and governs offer eligibility, stackability, and compliance — the rules and guardrails that ensure execution consistency across channels and customer touchpoints, and protect the commercial integrity of every offer in market.
  • Manages in-flight offer changes and edge-case escalations after launch — impact assessment, financial review, comms plan, and clear executive ownership for every change to a live commercial offer.

3 Channel & Ecosystem Strategy

  • Created B2B2C embedded distribution — designed a tri-party model placing connectivity inside third-party leasing workflows, capturing +2% market share at near-zero CAC and locking in building-level exclusivity that insulates the installed base from price competition.
  • Stood up digital self-service as a new commercial channel — Ooredoo’s first SMB digital sales motion, cutting digital order CAC by 50%, collapsing onboarding from 3 days to 8 hours, and earning the national Best Digital Transformation award.
  • Builds partner ecosystems and indirect channels — recruited and enabled 5 SOHO partner firms with 100+ frontline agents delivering sales and retention independently, with channel economics, deal registration, and tiered commercial terms that scale revenue without double-paying commissions.
  • Develops hyperscaler and strategic co-sell motions — Microsoft 365 attach playbooks that doubled M365 seats two years running, plus Google Workspace, Copilot, NotebookLM, IaaS, and managed-security stack co-sell.
  • Drives channel innovation — standing up new acquisition surfaces (embedded, self-service, partner-led managed services) that diversify the commercial mix and reduce dependency on traditional direct/retail/telemarketing motions.
  • Architects retention channels and post-sale ecosystems — renewal motions, win-back plays, and CX-led upsell paths that lifted partner NRR to 104% in a saturated market.

4 Revenue Operations & Commercial Governance

  • Designs adoption and activation strategy — first-use onboarding flows, time-to-value playbooks, and repeat-usage cadences that convert signups into contributing accounts and lock in second-purchase behavior where retention economics compound.
  • Runs post-launch optimization as an ongoing commercial program — funnel analytics across every step of the buying journey, conversion-rate testing at the highest-leverage drop-off points, abandonment analysis, and release prioritization driven by measured revenue and conversion lift.
  • Operates a weekly forecast governance cadence — the single operating rhythm of the commercial org — lifting forecast accuracy to 95% with 3.3× pipeline coverage and turning pipeline review into a repeatable performance ritual.
  • Builds funnel stage management and pipeline hygiene discipline — stage definitions, exit criteria, multithreading minimums, and late-stage management standards that keep the forecast credible.
  • Owns the annual operating plan and channel-level cascade — translating the board-approved number into channel commitments across field, retail, telemarketing, digital, partner, and embedded distribution, then to team and per-seller commitments.
  • Orchestrates Marketing, Sales, and CX alignment around shared revenue goals — common MQL/SQL/opportunity definitions, unified handoff protocols, and joint accountability frameworks.
  • Supporting: Compensation governance, territory design and capacity modeling, CRM administration, and sales methodology deployment (MEDDPICC, Challenger, Command of the Message) when required to make the commercial motion execute consistently.

5 AI-Enabled Commercialization

  • Deploys RAG-based AI sales assistants on GPT-4o integrated with the live tariff library, product catalog, regulatory documentation, and the PLM-governed CMS — functioning as a 24/7 enablement assistant for customers, reps, and partners.
  • Builds first-to-market B2B digital self-service platforms with guided CPQ, e-signature, and integrated RAG sales assistant — cutting digital order CAC by 50% and onboarding new customers in hours instead of days.
  • Stands up data monetization platforms — Marketing Campaigns as a Service, a usage-priced offer powered by anonymized subscriber data that generated $100K first-month revenue and set the precedent for compliant data monetization across the portfolio.
  • Builds AI-native commercialization content factories using CoPilot, Claude, and structured RAG — generating positioning, lead discovery banks, objection-and-response libraries, competitive teardowns, field guidance kits, and certification curricula at velocity previously impossible.
  • Designs commercial analytics and executive dashboards in Power BI, SQL, and Power Query — consolidating CRM, order, channel, and territory data into a single source of truth for revenue leadership and executive stakeholders.
  • Defines and operationalizes commercial KPIs — pipeline coverage, win rates, CAC, LTV, NRR, ARPA, ARPU, and time-to-close — translating raw performance data into actionable insights at every cadence.
  • Architects revenue automation and omnichannel data flows across orders, field reps, and retail shops — feeding CRM with current contacts, industry classification, and competitor intelligence; outbound conversion lifted 18% → 25%.
